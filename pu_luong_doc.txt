PLHDT – 02: BIKE TOURS: Local Market & Hidden Valley Discovery(Doan Market, Hieu Waterfall & Traditional Villages (Thu & Sun Only))
DOAN FAIR MARKET - HIEU VILLAGE– HIEU WATERFALL – DUCK FARM BROCADE WEAVING VILLAGE ( BY MOTORBIKE OR CAR) - Available on Thursdays & Sundays morning
DETAILED ITINERARY
7:30 am : Driver picks you up at Mist Valley Home departure to visit Doan fair market.
Doan has been the name of the market since the French colonial period in Lung Niem commune, Ba Thuoc district, Thanh Hoa province. This is the place to trade goods and meet the people of Kinh, Muong and Thai ethnic groups in the communes around the region with people in Tan Lac and Lac Son districts from Hoa Binh province.
The market is only open two times a week, on Thursdays, and Sundays, so it’s important to plan your visit accordingly. At the market, you can find a wide variety of goods, including fresh produce, handmade crafts, and traditional clothing. It’s a great place to shop for souvenirs and gifts, as many of the items sold at the market are unique to the area. In addition to shopping, the market is also a great place to try local cuisine. You’ll find a wide variety of street food vendors selling everything from traditional Vietnamese dishes to snacks and desserts.
8:30am: Continue to the Hieu village – Hieu waterfall
The driver continues to take you to the next spots – Hieu village – Hieu waterfall – Duck farm. Guests will follow the small trail winding through the rice terraces, gazing at the silhouette, house floor flickers. The waterfall is about 800m in length, with the stream falling from the top of the mountain.
The Hieu waterfall may have less water during the dry season (from November to April or even May of the following year); however, the cool and refreshing water will still be sufficient for you to soak in and cool off amidst the scorching weather.
Move to duck farm within Hieu village where you can see Cổ Lũng duck - a speciality breed of Pu Luong. The duck has a plump body, short legs, a large neck, and a plumage color similar to that of a sparrow, with a white border around the neck. The duck is low in fat and has a unique flavor. The meat is not tainted, and when boiled, is fragrant even without spices.
10:00am: Driver takes you to Lan Village – brocade weaving village
Lan Village was a place renowned for weaving traditional fabrics for the entire Pu Luong region. People from within a 70km radius would come here to exchange woven fabrics. They would then use these fabrics to tailor traditional clothing for their family members. For Thai women, beauty is not the most important aspect; what matters is the ability to weave clothes for their husbands and children and excel in farming. That's why when you come here, you only see women weaving fabrics.
11:00am: Driver takes you back Home/Resort. 
Journey's end.

PLFDT 02: BIKE/CAR + SHORT TREKKING TOURS: Pu Luong Signature Experience
(Water Wheels, Bamboo Raft Lunch, Hieu Waterfall & Local Villages)

LUNCH ON BAMBOO RAFT

 8:00 am: Driver picks you up at homestay/resort departure to the entrance of Chieng Lau village (11km) (by car/motorbike).
Chieng Lau village is the place where Thai Black people live, they live along streams with long-standing rice cultivation. To enter the village you have to cross the bridge: “suspension bridge” has just been built to help people here easily cross. You will walk through the rice fields to the village and meet the locals to learn about their life, culture and customs and reach the Cham stream and do bamboo rafting (2-3km). Glide peacefully on a handmade bamboo raft along the crystal-clear Cham Stream, surrounded by lush bamboo and towering mountains. This relaxing ride offers a unique glimpse into local life and the stunning natural beauty of Pù Luông. A simple yet unforgettable cultural experience. Come here, you can take a bath at Cham stream to cool off.
Note: you should prepare personal belongings avoid getting wet, or want to take a bath at Cham stream
9:30 am: Continue to visit Water wheel and ancient stilt house (hiking 2.5km)
The water wheel is the great invention of the Thai and Muong ethnic people here, marking the development of our own agricultural literature. They are handmade structures used for irrigation of farmland – mainly rice paddies. These clever machines lift water above river banks to provide sustenance to higher fields and homes. After the water is raised by the wheel it flows into long pieces of bamboo that are cut in half. The lengths of bamboo are used as aqueducts to redirect water to where it’s needed- it is all done without electricity, harnessing the natural energy of streams and rivers. Next, you’ll visit a traditional stilt house over 100 years old belonging to a local Thai ethnic family. Here, you’ll have the chance to meet the villagers and take part in their authentic daily activities — such as pounding rice using age-old wooden tools, husking rice, playing traditional games like "ném còn" (a throwing game) and archery
10:30 am: Continue trek to Cham stream, have lunch on a boat
 
1:30 pm: Driver picks you up to visit the duck farm – Hieu village – Hieu waterfall (20km by car/motorbike.
 
The driver takes you to Hieu Village and starts an easy trek. From there , follow the small road, trail through the terrace, rice fields, and jungle to reach Hieu Waterfall.
The waterfall is about 800m in length, with the stream falling from the top of the mountain. The waterfall has no mud and no moss. The water of this stream has a lot of limestone materials, creating solidifications between the background and the sides of the stream, causing the trees and objects to be petrified. At the foot of the waterfall, there is a swimming area with a water level of more than 1m, allowing visitors to freely play and swim. The Hieu waterfall may have less water during the dry season (from November to April or even May of the following year); however, the cool and refreshing water will still be sufficient for you to soak in and cool off amidst the scorching weather. Move to duck farm within Hieu village where you can see Cổ Lũng duck - a speciality breed of Pu Luong.The duck has a plump body, short legs, a large neck, and a plumage color similar to that of a sparrow, with a white border around the neck. The duck is low in fat and has a unique flavor. The meat is not tainted, and when boiled, is fragrant even without spices
3:00 pm: Continue trekking to the brocade weaving village at Uoi village and Lan village (8km easy trekking – estimate 2 hours)
At the foot of giant limestone mountains, surrounded by stretching terraced fields, and green palm hills lie the villages of Uoi and Lan. Lan Village was a place renowned for weaving traditional fabrics for the entire Pu Luong region. People from within a 70km radius would come here to exchange woven fabrics. Each traditional handicraft product has a story embedded in it and is a valuable folk cultural treasure of the Thai people residing here. They would then use these fabrics to tailor traditional clothing for their family members. For Thai women, beauty is not the most important aspect; what matters is the ability to weave clothes for their husbands and children and excel in farming. That's why when you come here, you only see women weaving fabrics
6:00 pm: Driver will pick you up at Uoi village and take you back to Homestay/Resort.
